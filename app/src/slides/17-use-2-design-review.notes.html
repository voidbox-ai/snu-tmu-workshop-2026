The second use is the one I find more interesting. We overlay our proposal directly onto the scan, and walk through it.

Which means the design gets judged inside the existing place, rather than against an abstracted model of it. Daylight, circulation, the relationship to what is already there — those stay visible the whole time, instead of being reconstructed from drawings in your head.

The user of the twin here is not a visitor. It is the designer. Same capability, completely different consumer.
