Good afternoon. I'm Hyeogjin Noh, from a company called voidbox. We build 3D scanning and spatial software for architecture.

Before voidbox — back in graduate school — I was already drawn to putting data to work in research. My master's was on population decline in Korea, and on a strategy for correctional facilities under those conditions.

It was during that work that I read Professor Aiba Shin's Toshi o Tatamu. I found myself agreeing with a great deal of it, and it has stayed with me since.

Since starting the company, taking on new things has mostly meant a steady run of failures. What I am going to show you today is one of the bigger ones.

I recently read Tadao Ando's Rensen Renpai — a book made out of the competitions he lost. He turned those defeats into lectures. If his losses could become a lecture, I would like mine to become a starting point for what comes next.

Today I want to walk you through two projects we ran this year, and be honest about which parts worked and which didn't.
