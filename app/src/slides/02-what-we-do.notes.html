First, what we actually do. We scan buildings and places using an algorithm called 3D Gaussian Splatting, and turn them into spaces you can walk through in a web browser.

Why that matters here: in Korea, places disappear constantly. A graduation exhibition lasts only five days. A neighborhood is redeveloped. And the digital twin is usually offered as the way to keep them.

But keep what, exactly? The space? What happened in it? Or the community itself — can a digital twin hold that? That is the question both of these projects were really about.
