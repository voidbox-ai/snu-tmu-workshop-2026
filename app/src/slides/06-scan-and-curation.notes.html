Here is what that actually involved. We scanned the hall before installation. Then we built a curation tool so the department could position each student's work inside the scanned space, working from a plan view — that is what you're looking at.

Around it we built the archive site: navigation, notices, a page for each work.

The point being that the virtual exhibition was not a video or a photo gallery. It was the hall itself, with the show installed in it.
