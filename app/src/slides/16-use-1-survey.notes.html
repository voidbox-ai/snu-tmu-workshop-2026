Two uses. The first is the ordinary one: existing-condition survey. This is a school built decades ago; the drawings are old and incomplete. The scan stands in for going out and measuring it.

And a technical note, since I said earlier that Gaussians are appearance and not geometry. That is true — so alongside them we extract a low-density point cloud. It is not survey-grade, and I would not hand it to a structural engineer. But it is dimensionable, which is what makes this usable in a real design process.
