To place it against the two things it usually gets confused with. A metaverse is an alternative space — parallel to the real one, not touching it. A digital twin is a mirror space — the real world writes into the virtual one, one direction only.

What we wanted was the third: causal coupling, both directions. The reasoning was that if the two spaces act on each other they become one event, and if they become one event, the people online and the people in the room become one community.

That was our bet.
